Mapping the Belt and Road Initiative

BRI Explorer is an interactive visualization of China's Belt and Road Initiative (BRI), launched in 2013. Explore participating countries, flagship infrastructure projects, about 1,300 Chinese finance records from AidData's research dataset, the latest engagement figures, the six economic corridors, and the land and maritime routes. Click a country or project to see details. Every figure is labelled with its source and shown as a reported estimate.
